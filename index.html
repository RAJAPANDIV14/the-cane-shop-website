Our Story

From cart to café.

We didn't start with a storefront. We started with a cart, a stalk of sugarcane, and a belief that Bodi deserved food made with real ingredients and real intent.

The Cart

It began simply — pure sugarcane juice, pressed to order, no shortcuts. No water. No ice. No added sugar. Just cane, and whatever fruit was in season.

Where it started

The Café

The cart grew into a full café — air-conditioned, seated, and serving a menu that travelled: Korean momos and buns, house pastries, and gelato made the slow way, alongside the juices that started it all.

Where we are now
